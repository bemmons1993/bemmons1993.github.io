## Clouds, like in the sky?

  The field of software engineering is currently undergoing massive transformations. We are moving toward dynamic, data-driven systems hosted in the cloud (someone else's computer). My primary interest lies in the sphere of cloud networking, data science, and artificial intelligence. The cloud is a constantly evolving network consisting of important connections powered by AI. To sharpen my blades in this area, I want to develop my skills to allow me to build and contribute to infrastructure while extracting useful information from various datasets.



## Relating Cloud Networking with Data Science

  In the future I hope to expand my knowledge and expertise in cloud native architecture, particularly in learning to design these virtual networks. Aside from basic functionality, really honing in and focusing on performance optimization will help me achieve moving data across the world as quickly as possible. Alongside these networking skills, I want to expand my expertise in data science. I can use what I learn in that field to help me understand overall trends that will help guide implementation of these types of services.



## Data Science -> AI -> Cloud Networking
     
  In the end, I'd like to utilize AI to help deploy cloud systems, AI that has been refined through various data models. Using machine learning to automate and improve these systems will certainly help me be effective in this goal. It's imperative to me to develop such things to operate on the highest orders of intelligence possible and not just something that has basic functioning. Keeping up with current technologies in this ever changing world we live in will help me make these ambitious outlooks a reality in the future.
