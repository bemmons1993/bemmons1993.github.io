The field of software engineering is currently undergoing big changes. We are moving away from static applications toward dynamic, data-driven systems hosted in the cloud. My primary interest lies in the crossroads of cloud networking, data science, and artificial intelligence. The cloud is not just a place to store data, but a constantly evolving network consisting of important connections powered by AI. To sharpen my blades in this area, I want to develop my skills to allow me to build and contribute to infrastructure while deriving useful information from datasets.
In the future my wish is to expand my knowledge and expertise in cloud native architecture, in particular learning to design these virtual networks. Aside from basic functionality, really honing in and focusing on performance optimization will help me achieve my goal that data will move across the world as quickly as possible. Alongside these networking skills, I aim to refine my abilities in data science. I can use what I learn to help build prediction models that can help me understand current trends which would help me maintain and operate larger scale distributed systems.
In the end, putting together these concepts will be achieved by deploying AI within cloud systems. Using machine learning to automate and improve these systems will certainly help me be effective in this goal. Utilizing the skills learned within software engineering, I can extend those to skills such as model training and automated cloud scaling. It's important to me to develop software that is genuinely intelligent to provide an efficient and also pleasant user experience. Contributing to such types of systems I believe will be relevant to the trajectory our technology is heading.
